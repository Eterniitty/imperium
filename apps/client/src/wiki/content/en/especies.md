# Species

Alien species are **not playable**. Their stance towards humanity is not uniform: within a single species there can be hostile and friendly factions (the **Great Hostility**, see [Lore](/wiki/lore#the-great-hostility)). In the game they are **data** (nature, doctrine, territory, fleets, ships and the behaviour of their AI), not hand-written rules: every figure in this article comes from that data.

Every species that has not vanished **already lives in the universe**: they have territory, fleets with their own ships and an AI that moves them (see [AI and territories](/wiki/ia-territorios)). The **Precursors** leave only ruins.

## At a glance

The more dangerous a species, the **farther from the Human Core** it lives. The ring is the distance in sectors (of 100 light years) from the Core: the Core is rings 0 and 1 and **nobody lives there**.

{{tabla:especies-resumen}}

- **Attack**: they send fleets against anyone who enters their territory if they are hostile to you.
- **Defend**: only their fleets already in the system you reach will fight.
- The **stance** decides whether they are hostile by nature. **Neutral** or **potential ally** factions only attack you if you give them reason (see [grudge and tribute](/wiki/ia-territorios#grudge-and-tribute)).

## Cards

Each card gathers its philosophy, its attitude towards humanity, where it lives, how it forms its fleets, how its AI behaves, its ships and its factions.

## Alien ships

Alien ships are not built in human shipyards: the AI deploys them in its territory. In [round-based combat](/wiki/guerra) they count just like human ones (hull, attack and battle shield).
