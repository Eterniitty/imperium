# Species

Alien species are **not playable**. Their stance towards humanity is not uniform: within a single species there can be hostile and friendly factions (the **Great Hostility**, see [Lore](/wiki/lore#the-great-hostility)). In the game they are **data** (nature, doctrine, expansion, stance, willingness to make pacts, preferences and traits), not hand-written rules.

> [!dev] Today only the **Scavengers** act in the game, as AI pirates (see [Combat](/wiki/combate#scavengers)). The rest will arrive with faction AI (phase 11). Faction names are provisional.
