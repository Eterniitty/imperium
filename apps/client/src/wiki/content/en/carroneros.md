# Scavengers

Pirates of mixed species (faction **Rust Tide**): they raid anyone who ventures outside the **Human Core**, which they never enter. They do not fight for honour: they want loot at the lowest risk. They appear in two ways:

- **in local space**, as AI-flown ships that attack you in [real-time combat](/wiki/combate) while you fly your ship;
- **as fleets** in their territory, fighting your fleets in rounds (see [AI and territories](/wiki/ia-territorios) and [War](/wiki/guerra)).

## Their ships

{{tabla:carroneros-naves}}

- **Skiff**: a cockpit welded to an engine; fast and disposable, it comes in swarms.
- **Raider**: the classic pirate; it circles its prey at medium range.
- **Harpooner**: it latches on at point-blank range with magnetic harpoons and boarding pods.
- **Bomber**: it stays far away and fires missiles; if you close in, it backs off.
- **Breaker**: a freighter armoured with hammer blows, with turrets and missiles.
- **Mothership**: a colossal freighter turned lair; it leads the pack in the most dangerous zones.

Each one has its own scrap model (asymmetric plates, spikes, engines of different sizes, reddish lights) and fires from its own hardpoints.

> [!dev] Boarding (stealing cargo instead of destroying the ship) will come with the logistics of conquest; for now the harpooner just hits hard at close range.

## How they fight

Scavengers look for loot with the least risk:

- **They hunt in packs**: those in a system pick **the same prey**, the ship that can take the least (hull and shield) near any of them, and go for it together.
- **They surround you**: from afar, each one comes in from its own side (a pincer), so you cannot face them all at once.
- **They jink** sideways when they are being hit, to throw off your aim.
- **They harass, they do not die for it**: with low hull and under fire they **flee**; far from you they **regroup** until their shield recharges and come back hunting.
- **They do not crash**: they steer around planets and moons and stay out of the star's heat zone.
- **They do not shoot each other**: they hold fire when a packmate is in the line of fire.

### Ambushes at jump exits

When you leave the rift in a system with Scavengers, sometimes (35 % of arrivals) the pack is **already waiting** a few kilometres from the arrival point. Start your engines and ready your weapons as soon as you arrive.

### Raids on routes

The Rust Tide lives off raiding supply lines. When a convoy on an **automatic route** reaches a system outside the Human Core, sometimes a **raiding party** of 2 to 4 ships jumps it and a fleet battle starts. If it loses, the convoy loses its cargo. An **armed escort** in the fleet makes raids much less likely. If nobody destroys it, the party leaves after a couple of hours.

### Lairs

In some Scavenger systems far from the Core there is a **lair**: an **anchored** Scavenger mothership that does not move, very tough and well armed. It never flees. If you destroy it, the Scavengers of that system **stop respawning** for a few hours, and the lair takes a day to come back.

## Danger by zone

The farther from the Core, the more numerous, the heavier and the better their aim. The ring is the distance to the Core in sectors (see [Universe](/wiki/universo)).

{{tabla:carroneros}}

They only chase **piloted** ships: your parked ship does not interest them. Destroyed ones come back after a while.

## Their fleets

The fleets in their territory also change with depth: skiffs and raiders at the edge; breakers, bombers and the odd mothership in the deepest regions.

## Hunt challenge

If you want to test yourself against them, the **Goals** tab of the contracts window has the **hunt challenge**: flying **outside the Human Core**, in a system with Scavengers, press **Start here**. The system's pack appears all around you and, each time it falls completely, another **wave** arrives. At the top centre you see the wave, your kills and the time left. If you leave the system, park or fall, the challenge ends with what you have. When it ends you are paid for each kill and your **record** is saved.
