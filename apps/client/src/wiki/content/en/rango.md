# Emperor's rank

Everything you do in the galaxy gives you **experience**, and with it you rise in **rank**: from Aspirant to Aeonarch. Your title appears in the top bar, next to your name, and the details are in the **Rank** tab of the [contracts](/wiki/contratos) window.

## How to earn experience

{{tabla:rango-experiencia}}

What happens while the tab is closed also counts: a fleet or a probe that discovers a system gives you experience just like your ship. Experience is **never lost**, not even when you fall in combat: your [lineage](/wiki/linaje) keeps it.

## Ranks and improvements

{{tabla:rangos}}

Improvements add up: each rank keeps those of the ranks before it. The server applies them as soon as you rise, including to today's offers you have not accepted yet.

> [!dev]
> More improvements will come with rank (hold, fuel, missiles) and more ways to earn experience (space events and daily goals).
