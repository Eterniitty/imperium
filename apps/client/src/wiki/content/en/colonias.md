# Claiming planets and colonies

## The planet window

Choosing a planet (in the system list or by clicking on it) opens its **window**, with tabs:

- **Planet**: type, orbit, size, habitability, moons, deposits, owner; claim, rename or besiege it and, while piloting, cruise to it or orbit it.
- If the colony is yours, also: **Population** (inhabitants, energy, upkeep, landing and boarding humans), **Resources** (stock, hourly production, components and unloading your ship's hold), **Installations** (current ones and building), **Production** (build queue and crafting) and **Ships** (shipyard).

The **▁** button minimises it into a tab under the top bar (click to expand it) and **×** closes it.

## Claiming a planet

A **free** planet in a system **you have discovered** can be claimed in two ways:

1. **With your ship**: your Emperor must be in that system and not jumping. You choose how many humans to land (at least the [colonists needed](/wiki/humanos#how-many-colonists-you-need) for that planet and at most as many as you carry).
2. **With a colonizer**: a fleet of yours stopped in that system and not in combat, with a **Colonizer** carrying enough colonists. **All** its colonists land and the colonizer **is dismantled** to found the colony; if the fleet is left without ships, it disappears.

Select the planet in the system panel and press **Claim**. If two Emperors claim it at the same time, only one succeeds; the other gets "That planet already has an owner".

## What you get

- The **planet's resources** (metal, volatiles and crystal according to its richness) and **half of its moons'**.
- The **energy of its star**.
- A place to **build installations and ships**.

All of this is explained with figures in [Economy](/wiki/economia).

## Renaming

Press **Rename** on your colony: 2 to 32 characters (letters, digits, spaces, hyphens and apostrophes). Anyone who has discovered the system sees the name, including on the planet's label in the 3D view.

## The capital

Your **capital** is your **oldest** colony. Besides its own upkeep, **it pays for all your ships**. See [Upkeep](/wiki/economia#upkeep).

## Who sees what

- **Owner and name** of each claimed planet: anyone who has discovered the system.
- **Population, stock, production, installations and queue**: only you.

## Independent colonies

If a player **deletes their account**, their planets do not vanish: they remain as **independent colonies**, with the names they were given. Whatever had been produced up to then stays, their orders are cancelled (with the usual refund) and from then on they are **frozen**: they neither produce nor consume. The panel shows their owner as **"Independent"**, and they cannot be claimed.

Independent colonies **can be conquered** like any other: see [Conquest](/wiki/conquista). Once taken they produce again from that moment (they do not collect the time they spent frozen).
