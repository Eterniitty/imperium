# Contracts

**Contracts** are jobs with a reward: they tell you what to do now and pay you when you complete them. Open them with the button in the top bar (the number is how many you have under way; the gold dot means offers are left).

## How they work

{{tabla:contratos-reglas}}

- Every **game day** you get new offers, just for you. The ones you don't accept disappear when the day changes.
- You can only hold a limited number of contracts at a time.
- Accepting one starts its **deadline**. The server counts progress from what you already do in the game, even with the tab closed: a fleet or a probe that discovers a system counts.
- When you complete it you get the **credits** in your wallet, **reputation** with humanity (see [Goals](/wiki/metas#reputation)) and **experience** for your [rank](/wiki/rango), which in turn gives more slots, more offers and bigger rewards.
- If the deadline runs out, it **expires** with no reward. You can **abandon** it whenever you like, also with no reward, to free the slot.

## Kinds

{{tabla:contratos}}

- **Scavenger hunt**: every pirate you shoot down counts (see [Scavengers](/wiki/carroneros)).
- **Exploration**: every system you discover for the first time counts, with your ship, your fleets or your probes (see [Fog of war](/wiki/niebla)).
- **Mining**: the ore you extract with the laser in deep space counts (see [Deep space and mining](/wiki/espacio-profundo)).
- **Rescue**: when you accept it, the contract places some **drifting wreckage** in one of the nearest systems you already know. A gold marker shows it in that system and it appears as a [cruise](/wiki/pilotar) destination; the **Show on the map** button takes you to its system. You have to fly there and stay next to it for a few seconds.
