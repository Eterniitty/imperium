# Space events

While you fly your ship, the galaxy comes to meet you: on arrival in a system or from time to time, your sensors pick up something nearby. A notice appears at the bottom centre of the screen and, in the system, an always-visible **blue marker**, which also shows up as a destination in the [cruise](/wiki/pilotar) list.

## Kinds

- **Drifting wreckage**: wreckage with cargo. Reach the marker, stay next to it for a moment and the ore goes to your [hold](/wiki/espacio-profundo) (whatever fits).
- **Anomaly**: something unexplained. Reach the marker and stay for the length of the **scan**: you take **exotic matter** to the hold and experience.
- **Distress signal**: a ship is calling for help. You choose **Respond** or **Ignore**. If you respond and get there, you rescue it and earn credits, reputation and experience. Far from the Human Core, if there are [Scavengers](/wiki/carroneros) in the system, it is sometimes a **trap**: the pack appears all around you.
