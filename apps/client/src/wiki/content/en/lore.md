# Lore

## An ancient universe

The universe is ancient. So ancient that stars have been born and died countless times, entire civilisations have risen from primordial oceans and vanished without a trace, and some regions of the cosmos hold the ruins of species whose existence predates even today's galaxies.

No one knows the limits of the universe. No one knows its ultimate origin. And no one knows for certain whether anything exists beyond it.

The six primordial gods are dead.

Among all the forms of life that have appeared in the cosmic vastness there is an anomaly that contradicts everything other civilisations believe they understand about existence: **humanity**. Humans are not the most intelligent species, nor the oldest, nor the most advanced. But they have something no other species has managed to reproduce: **they cannot be exterminated for good**. A human can die; an empire can burn. But humanity always returns.

## The Emperor

There is no single Emperor: each player embodies one. Emperors are humans able to persist through death. Their bodies are mortal, their memory can be damaged and everything they build can be destroyed, but their existence does not depend entirely on a single organism. They can also give rise to new humans without any existing population.

In Aeonarch, the word Emperor **is a category of existence**. An Emperor can start with no territory, no army and no allies, rule millions of people, lose them all… and carry on. Their rules are in [The three laws of the Emperor](/wiki/leyes).

## The Great Hostility

Humanity seems to have been born with an answer to death that no one has managed to explain, and that makes it a threat. To some species it is an abomination; to others, a disease that cannot be eradicated; others believe the Emperors hide a power able to break the balance of the cosmos.

So the **Great Hostility** was born: a conflict with no declaration of war and no single enemy. Some civilisations attack out of fear; others want to study, enslave or exploit human persistence; others want to wipe out every trace of humanity. But there are also species that trade with the Emperors, make pacts with them or fight alongside them.

The war is universal, but not everyone in the universe is an enemy. Humanity is not fighting to win a war: **it is fighting to keep its right to exist**. Who is who, in [Species](/wiki/especies).

## The Aeonarch era

No one remembers when the first Emperor appeared. The oldest human archives are fragmentary; some Emperors remember lives that appear in no record, others wake up with no memory, surrounded by unknown technology.

Over time, the Emperors built shelters, stations, colonies, industrial worlds, fortresses and fleets. But no definitive human empire ever arose: each Emperor followed their own path, and the universe learned that destroying a human empire is not the same as destroying humanity.

**Aeonarch** is the name of the era in which this struggle stopped being a series of isolated wars and became a permanent condition of existence. It is not the name of a kingdom but of an age: one in which every Emperor can raise an empire, and every empire can fall.
