# Account and data

## Signing in without a password

- You sign in with a **link sent to your email**: it is **single-use** and **expires after 15 minutes**. If you did not ask for it, ignore it: without the link nobody can get into your account.
- When you open it, the game asks you to confirm **Sign in**. If another session was already open in that browser, it warns you that it will be closed.
- The session lasts **30 days** in that browser (a technical cookie). **Sign out** is in Settings → Account.
- To request a link you must be **at least 16** and accept the **terms of use** and the **privacy policy**.
- Attempts are limited: if you request too many links in a row, wait a few minutes.

## Your data

In **Settings → Account**:

- **Download my data**: a JSON file with everything stored about your account and your game (account, Emperor, fleets and ships, cargo, routes, colonies, installations, orders, discoveries, companions, battles, your credit wallet, your contributions, [market](/wiki/mercado) orders, trades and shipments, and your entries in the [resource ledger](/wiki/economia#resource-ledger)).
- **Delete my account**: type **DELETE** to confirm. Your account and email, your Emperor, your fleets, your discoveries and your companion list are deleted immediately (other players' accounts are not touched). **It cannot be undone.**
- What you did in the universe **stays**: your planets keep the names you gave them, as [independent colonies](/wiki/colonias#independent-colonies). Your market orders are cancelled first (whatever was held returns to your colonies) and your contributions to common projects stay, anonymous.

## Cookies and privacy

There are only **strictly necessary technical cookies** (your session cookie and Cloudflare's protection cookies): no advertising, analytics or tracking. Aeonarch is **free and not monetised**, and it only emails you when you request a link.

The legal details (controller, data processed, retention periods, backups and your rights) are on the legal pages: [imprint](/legal/imprint.html), [privacy](/legal/privacy.html) and [terms of use](/legal/terms.html).

## Game settings

They are stored **in your browser** (not on the server): language, image quality, interface size, **music** volume (generated by code in your browser, with no audio files) and [keys](/wiki/pilotar#controls).

## Wiki administrators

Administrator accounts (decided by the server) see an **Edit** button on every Wiki article and **New article** in the index:

- Markdown editor with a **live preview**, in Spanish or English (it warns when one language has fallen behind the other), an **edit note** and the list of **game tables** to insert (`{{tabla:<id>}}`).
- **History**: every publication is a revision that is never deleted; you can see how it differs from the published version and **restore** any earlier version or the original text.
- Articles can be **hidden** or moved to another index group.

If an administrator account is deleted, its revisions are kept (they are public Wiki text) without an author; your data download includes the ones you made.
