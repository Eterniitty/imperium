# Technology

Your empire researches **five branches** of technology, with **credits** and time, **one at a time**. It opens in the **Humanity** panel, **Research** tab. Each level improves something in your empire and unlocks **parts and hulls** in the [Shipyard](/wiki/astillero): in the Shipyard, what you cannot build yet carries a padlock 🔒.

## The branches

{{tabla:tecnologia-ramas}}

Weapons and shields improvements apply in **round-based fleet battles** (see [War](/wiki/guerra)); propulsion, in **fleet travel** between stars; quantum drive, in their **fuel** (see [Fleets](/wiki/flotas)); and industry, in the time of what you order in your colonies.
