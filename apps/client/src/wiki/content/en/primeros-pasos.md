# First steps

## Signing in

1. On the start page, enter your **email address**, tick the box (you are at least 16 and accept the terms of use and the privacy policy) and press **Send sign-in link**.
2. You receive an email with a single-use link that **expires after 15 minutes**. Open it in the same browser and press **Sign in**. There are no passwords.
3. The first time, **name your Emperor** (3 to 24 characters: letters, digits, spaces, hyphens and apostrophes). Other players will see this name and it **cannot be changed**. Press **Crown**.
   - To start next to a friend, also enter their Emperor's name under **Start near a companion** and press **Send request**. Once they accept, you start near their empire (see [Companions](/wiki/companeros)).
4. Before you play, just once, the **photosensitivity warning** appears (the game has flashes): you can turn on **Reduce visual effects** or continue. You can see it again and change it in Settings → General. Even without it, the game always **limits flashes**: at most three strong flashes per second (the rest are heavily dimmed), none goes from dark to full at once, and HUD warnings pulse gently instead of blinking.
5. Press **Awaken**: you start at the controls of your ship, inside your home system.

More about sessions and your data in [Account and data](/wiki/cuenta).

## What you start with

- **Your ship**, the Emperor's. You always travel in it: you fly it, jump between stars and fight with it. Its figures are in [Combat](/wiki/combate#the-emperor-s-ship).
- **Humans on board**, which your ship generates by itself over time ([Law II](/wiki/humanos)). You found colonies with them.
- **A starting fleet** ("Fleet 1") with an Explorer, in your system.
- A **home system** picked at random among the Sun-like stars (classes F, G and K) of the **Human Core**, humanity's central region (if you start with a companion, one of the closest to theirs). You already know that system and every system within your scanners' reach (see [Fog of war](/wiki/niebla)).

## The screen

- **Top bar**: your Emperor (press it to centre the map on them; while travelling, the destination and time left), the **humans** on board, the **Map / Pilot** button ([[M]]), the map's sector and position, a **Go** field to move the camera to a sector by coordinates, the server status, the link to this **Wiki**, **Settings** (⚙) and FPS.
- **Side panel**: the selected system (star, planets, resources, who is there), your fleets, your colonies and your battles.
- **Settings** (⚙): language, image quality, interface size and music (*General*); configurable keys (*Controls*); [companions and privacy](/wiki/companeros) (*Companions*); and [your account](/wiki/cuenta) (*Account*).

## Your first minutes

1. **Fly around** your system: [[W]] to move forward, drag with the mouse to aim the ship, [[Shift]] to boost and [[P]] for the pulse drive between planets. See [Flying](/wiki/pilotar).
2. Press [[M]] to open the **galaxy map**. Click your star and look at its planets in the panel: type, habitability and resources.
3. **Claim a planet** in your system by landing humans from your ship. A world with good habitability needs fewer colonists. See [Colonies](/wiki/colonias).
4. In the colony, order a **mine** or a **power plant**. See [Economy](/wiki/economia).
5. Send your **Explorer** to a nearby star to lift the fog of war (select it on the map and press *Send Fleet 1 here*).
6. When you are ready, **jump** with your ship to another star: select it and press [[J]].

> [!nota] Outside the Human Core there are **Scavenger** pirates and, farther out, [alien territories](/wiki/ia-territorios). There is none of that in the Core. See [Combat](/wiki/combate).
