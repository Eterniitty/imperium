# Goals: prestige and ranking

Your empire earns **prestige** from what it achieves. The **Ranking** tab (in the **Humanity** panel) shows your place among all human Emperors, players and AI, the **goals of the era** with their progress and the table of the top fifty.

## How prestige is earned

{{tabla:prestigio}}

The great feat is **reaching Omphalos**, the supermassive black hole at the centre of the galaxy (see [Universe](/wiki/universo#the-galaxy)): it counts as soon as you fly your ship in its system. Whoever achieves it carries the ◉ badge in the ranking.

## Reputation

Your **reputation with humanity** goes from −100 to 100 and shows in the Ranking tab:

- It **rises** by downing Scavengers (much more for a **lair**) and by contributing **credits** to humanity's common [projects](/wiki/humanidad).
- It **falls** by attacking other humans in the **Human Core**.
- With a good reputation, AI Emperors **accept pacts** (becoming [companions](/wiki/companeros)): merchants almost always, expansionists if it is not negative and **militarists** only if it is very good.

## Privacy

The ranking shows the **name** of public Emperors, AI ones, your mutual companions and yourself. The rest appear as **Hidden Emperor**, with their prestige but no name (see [Companions](/wiki/companeros)). The table updates at most once a minute.

## Galactic crises

From time to time the whole galaxy goes through a **crisis** lasting a few hours, announced in the top bar (⚠). Between one and the next there are hours of calm.

## Legends of the era

The **first** Emperor to achieve each great feat is inscribed forever in the Ranking tab and earns **legacy prestige**: reaching Omphalos, an empire of ten colonies, an armada of fifty ships or charting two thousand systems. Only players count.
