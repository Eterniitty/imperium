# Aeonarch Wiki

Aeonarch is a space MMO in the browser. You are an **immortal Emperor**: you fly your ship through a practically endless universe, discover systems, claim planets, build an economy, haul resources in freighters and fight pirates and other Emperors. The universe is shared and **runs in real time**: whatever you build keeps producing while you are away.

> [!nota] The game is in **alpha**. This Wiki describes what is in the code today; anything that does not exist yet is marked **In development**. The figures in the tables are read straight from the game's data, so they are always up to date.
