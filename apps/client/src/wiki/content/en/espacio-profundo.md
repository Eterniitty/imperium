# Deep space and mining

Not everything between the stars is empty. During a [quantum jump](/wiki/pilotar#quantum-travel-to-another-star) you can **drop out of the tunnel halfway** and stay in **deep space**, where there are sometimes asteroid fields your ship can mine: ice to make fuel, metals, rare metals and even **exotic matter**. All figures on this page are read from the game code.

## Dropping out of the tunnel

- In the quantum tunnel, press [[K]] or the **Drop out here** button. The ship stops at the point on the line between the two systems that matches how far you had travelled.
- **You only spend the fuel for the distance covered**: the rest of the jump goes back into the tank.
- You are left at the controls in a **deep space region**. Space is divided into cells; each cell is a region with its own contents. **Anyone who drops out in the same region shares it with you**: you see each other and can mine (or fight) together. Privacy and the [fog of war](/wiki/niebla) work as in star systems; on arrival, your scanner discovers the nearby stars.
- To leave, press [[J]] and pick a destination within your fuel range (including the one you had before, to **resume** the trip), or pick a star on the map.

## What a region holds

What each region holds is decided by a secret server layer (like system details): you cannot know in advance. Many are empty; others have asteroid fields, the wreckage of forgotten battles or anomalies.

{{tabla:asteroides}}

**Exotic matter** is the scarcest: it appears in fragments where space still keeps the imprint of what the dead gods were and of the Precursors' ruins. **Rare metals** (platinum, iridium, osmium, neodymium…) come mostly from metallic and rich asteroids.

An asteroid **is depleted** as you mine it (it looks smaller and duller) and **regenerates on its own over time**. The server keeps track of what was mined: if another Emperor depletes it, you will see it depleted.

## Mining

1. Get close to an asteroid until it is **within range** (the HUD shows it in green).
2. Hold [[L]] while aiming at the asteroid: the **mining laser** extracts a few units per second and uses reactor energy. It does not work with the pulse drive on.
3. What you extract goes into your ship's **hold**, which is small. When it is full, the laser stops.

## Refining and unloading

- **Refine fuel**: in the hold panel, the **Refine fuel** button slowly turns the volatiles in the hold into fuel for the tank. It yields less than refuelling at a colony, but lets you keep travelling without going home.
- **Unload**: in a system with one of your colonies, open its planet and press **Unload the hold here**. What you mined goes into its stock (whatever does not fit in storage stays aboard) and is recorded in the [resource ledger](/wiki/economia#resource-ledger).
- **Rare metals** and **exotic matter** can only be obtained this way or on the [market](/wiki/mercado). Capital ships need them (see [Ships](/wiki/naves)) and you can sell them to other Emperors; the Nomads buy rare metals.
