# Companions and privacy

## Companions

Two Emperors are **companions** when **both have added each other**. You manage this in **Settings → Companions**:

- **Add by Emperor name** (case-insensitive). Until the other player adds you, they show as "Waiting for them to add you".
- **They added you**: players who have added you; press **Add** to become companions or **Decline**.
- **Remove** a companion to end the relationship.

Being companions means:

- In [real-time combat](/wiki/combate#against-other-emperors-and-friendly-fire) **there is friendly fire**: your shots hurt each other if they hit. You are marked as "companion" on screen (cyan circle) and the aim assist does not lock you when cycling targets.
- Your fleets **do not fight each other** and do not show up as [contacts](/wiki/guerra#contacts-and-interception).
- With the "Only my companions" visibility, they can see where you are.

**AI Emperors**: you can ask them too. Expansionists and merchants accept at once; militarists never do, and nobody accepts while there is a siege under way between you.

## Starting next to a companion

When you **name your Emperor** (only when signing up) you can ask to start near someone who already plays:

1. Enter their Emperor's name under **Start near a companion** and press **Send request**. It only works with another player's Emperor (not the AI's).
2. They get a **Companion request** in the game with your name: **Accept** or **Decline**.
3. While you wait you can close the game and come back: the start page keeps waiting. You can also **withdraw the request** (and try someone else) or **start alone** with the name you chose.
4. If they **accept**, your Emperor is crowned automatically and starts in one of the starting systems (F, G or K stars of the Human Core) **closest to theirs**. You are also **companions** already.
5. If they **decline**, or do not answer within **24 hours**, you are told and can start alone or try another companion.

Your Emperor's name is not reserved while you wait: if someone takes it first, you will have to choose another (the accepted request is kept).

## Jumping next to a companion

If a companion **is** in a system, or **was** there, you can arrive **beside them** when you jump there: in the jump selector ([[J]]) or in the system panel on the map, choose **"Next to …"**. You arrive next to their ship (if it is still there) or at the last place they were. Without that option, you arrive where you yourself last were in that system (or at its edge). See [Flying the ship](/wiki/pilotar).

- Only between companions (you have both added each other), and not with someone who chose that **nobody** sees where they are.

## Who sees where you are

In **Settings → Companions → Who sees where you are** you choose who can see your Emperor's position on the map (where they are and where they are travelling to):

| Option | Who sees you |
|---|---|
| **Everyone** | Any player. |
| **Only my companions** (default) | Your companions. |
| **No one** | Nobody but you. |

So that the game can hold many players, on the map you only see the Emperors **near** your ship (within the distance in the table); when you move, the list updates. You always see your companions, wherever they are.

{{tabla:interes}}

- The filter is applied **on the server**: your position is **not even sent** to anyone who cannot see you.
- **Your fleets are only visible to you.**
- If you fly in the same system as another player, you see each other's ships: you are in the same place.
- To fly alongside a companion inside a system, the **autopilot** follows their ship in formation ([[H]]; see [Flying the ship](/wiki/pilotar)).

> [!dev] A future scanner will be able to detect anyone nearby. **Organisations** (with shared discoveries) will arrive in phase 12.

## Calling for help

While you pilot your ship, the **"Call for help"** button alerts your **online companions**: they see "… calls for help in …" at the top with a button to **jump to your side** (the normal jump, with its charge and fuel). If you are **near the Human Core**, the **militia** also sends reinforcements that fly in within seconds, fight the Scavengers and the aliens hostile to you and, after a while, return to their patrol. Then you have to wait before calling again.
