# Alien ships

Alien species are not built in human shipyards: their ships are deployed by the AI in their territory (see [AI and territories](/wiki/ia-territorios)). They fight under the same rules as human ships in [round-based battles](/wiki/guerra), with their attack, shield, hull and target size (from their length, unless their sheet says otherwise).

## Visual identity

Every ship is generated in 3D from its hull shape, length and engines, with the **style** and **colours** of its species, so it can be told apart at a glance:

| Style | Species | Look |
|---|---|---|
| **Angular** | Eradicators, Void Nomads, Aurelians | Tall dorsal fins, side blades and coloured edges. |
| **Geometric** | Greys, Vesperi | Perfect, symmetric shapes, polished metal and rings with light bands. |
| **Crystalline** | Elydri | Translucent hulls with crystal spines and a glowing core; no visible engines. |
| **Organic** | Tharaxi | Swollen hulls with ribs and glowing pustules; the engines are sacs of light. |
| **Ethereal** | Nihil, Precursors | A dark hull inside a light-bending halo, with segments floating loose. |
| **Scrap** | [Scavengers](/wiki/carroneros) | Asymmetric bolted plates, spikes and engines of different sizes. |

Human ships have their own style: a light panelled hull, coloured stripes and blue flames (see [Human ships](/wiki/naves)).

## In local space

The ships of alien fleets waiting in a system **come out in 3D** when you fly there (the biggest ones, up to the cap in the table). They fly with the same pilot as the [Scavengers](/wiki/carroneros#how-they-fight): in packs, surrounding their prey, jinking and fleeing with low hull.

- **They attack you if their faction is hostile to you** (by nature or by grudge; see [AI and territories](/wiki/ia-territorios)). Otherwise they leave you alone.
- **If you shoot them**, that ship and its pack come after you from then on, and their faction holds a **grudge**.
- Your [escorts](/wiki/pilotar#escorts) also attack hostile alien ships.
- Whatever you destroy, their fleet really loses.
