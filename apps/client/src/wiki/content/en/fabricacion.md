# Manufacturing

Colonies with a **Factory** turn resources into **components**: hull plates, circuits, power cells, optics, superconductors and exotic cores. The **advanced parts** in the [Shipyard](/wiki/astillero) (fusion reactors, B–D quantum drives, fleet and siege shields, lasers, lances, armour…) and the large hulls need them as well as resources and credits.

## How to manufacture

1. Build a **Factory** in the colony (see [Colonies](/wiki/colonias)).
2. In the colony panel, under **Factory**, order a **batch** of a component. It goes into the colony's **queue** like any other construction and is paid when ordered.
3. When finished, the components go into the **colony's inventory** ("Components").
4. Some recipes are **chains**: superconductors need circuits, and exotic cores need superconductors and power cells.

If you cancel a batch you get the resources and components back (part of them, if it had started).

## Who needs them

When you **build a design** from the Shipyard, the components come from the inventory of the colony where it is built: the Shipyard shows them in green if that colony has them and in red if not. Small parts need no components, so you can start without a Factory. The full list is in the hull and part tables of the [Shipyard](/wiki/astillero).

## Taking them to another colony

If you manufacture in one colony and build in another, carry the components in a **fleet's hold**: in the fleet's logistics, next to one of your colonies in the same system, each component has **Load** and **Unload** (everything of that type). They take up hold space like resources.
