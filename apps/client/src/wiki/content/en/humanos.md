# Humans and Law II

**Humans** are a resource like any other, and the most important one: without them no colony can be founded. Under [Law II](/wiki/leyes#ii-continuity), an Emperor can give rise to new humans without any existing population.

## Aboard the Emperor's ship

- Your ship **generates humans by itself**, little by little, **up to its capacity**. The current number is shown in the top bar (*Humans*: aboard / capacity).
- You use them to **found colonies** (landing them on a free planet) or to **reinforce** one of your colonies in the system where you are (the colony's *Disembark* button, without exceeding its planet's population cap).
- To land them, your ship must be in that system and **not jumping**.

## How many colonists you need

The more hostile a planet, the more colonists it takes to found a colony: `50 + 150 × (1 − habitability)`. A garden world needs 50; an airless rock, about 200. **How many humans you carry decides what you can colonise.**

Each planet also has a **population cap** that grows with habitability. The minimum cap always covers the colonists needed to found, so no colony is born above what its planet can hold.

{{tabla:colonos}}

## Colony population

- A colony's population **grows by itself** logistically towards its cap: fast at first, slower as it nears the cap (at 2 % per hour, it roughly doubles in a day and a half). If for some reason it is above the cap, it falls towards it.
- Population is **labour**: with 500 inhabitants installations work at full output; with fewer, more slowly (never below 25 %). See [Economy](/wiki/economia#labour).

## Moving humans

- **Colonizer**: when it is built in a shipyard, colonists from the colony board it (up to its capacity), **always leaving 50** behind. It founds a new colony on another planet and is consumed. See [Colonies](/wiki/colonias).
- **Freighter**: has **passenger seats** to move humans between your colonies, by hand or on an automatic route. When loading, the colony always keeps 50 inhabitants. See [Fleets and logistics](/wiki/flotas).

> [!dev] **Troops** (humans who land to take other players' planets) will arrive with conquest (phase 9b).
