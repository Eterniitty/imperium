What other players can see

Your Emperor's name is public. Where your Emperor is on the map is visible only to those you allow (everyone, only your allies, or nobody, under Settings → Allies); within the same star system, anyone present there can see your ship. The owner and name of a claimed planet are visible to anyone who has discovered that system. Your e-mail address is never shown to other players.

4. Who we share data with (processors)

  • Hetzner Online GmbH (Gunzenhausen, Germany): server hosting, in Germany.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. (San Francisco, USA): content delivery network, encryption and protection against attacks. All traffic to the site passes through Cloudflare, which sees your IP. Cloudflare is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses also apply, as set out in its data processing addendum (DPA).
  • Sendinblue SAS (Brevo) (Paris, France): sending the e-mails with the sign-in links. It receives your e-mail address and the content of the message.

We have data processing agreements under Art. 28 GDPR with all of them (or accept theirs).

5. How long we keep data

  • Account and game data: until you delete your account. While the game is in development, game data may be reset.
  • Sign-in links: valid for 15 minutes and deleted at the latest one day after they expire.
  • Sessions: 30 days, or until you sign out.
  • Technical server logs: 14 days.
  • Backups: one daily copy of the database, stored on the server itself (in Germany) for 14 days. So after you delete your account, your data may remain in those backups for up to 14 days; they are only used to restore the service after a failure.
  • Game resource ledger: resource transactions are kept for the integrity of the game, linked only to internal identifiers; once you delete your account they can no longer be traced back to you.

6. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), portability (Art. 20) and objection to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21).

  • You can download your data and delete your account yourself in the game: Settings → Account. Deletion is immediate and includes your email, your Emperor, fleets, discoveries and your list of allies (other players' accounts are not affected). What you did in the game universe stays, with no link to you: your planets become independent colonies and keep the names you gave them. If a name contains your personal data, write to us and we will change it.
  • For any other request, write to the e-mail address in section 1.
  • You can lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. The one responsible for us is the Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany (www.lda.bayern.de).

7. Minimum age

To create an account you must be at least 16 years old.

8. Security

All communication is encrypted (HTTPS). We do not store passwords; sign-in links and sessions are stored only as cryptographic hashes (they cannot be used even if someone read the database).

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, if the change is significant, ask you to accept it again when you sign in.
